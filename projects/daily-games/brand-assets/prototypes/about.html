How to play each game

WordBridge

A daily word-grouping game with a twist.

  1. Sort 16 words into 4 groups of 4.
  2. 4 mistakes allowed; group colors show difficulty.
  3. Then guess the secret bridge theme connecting all groups for a bonus star.
Play WordBridge
Numlock

A reverse sudoku of sums.

  1. Delete numbers so every row and column adds up to its target.
  2. Each puzzle has exactly one solution.
  3. Pure logic — no guessing needed.
Play Numlock
InfiniteAlchemy

AI-powered crafting.

  1. Combine two elements to invent a new one.
  2. Reach today’s target element in as few steps as you can.
  3. Anything can combine — the AI decides what you make.
Play InfiniteAlchemy
GridSpark

A crown-placement logic puzzle.

  1. Place exactly one crown in every row, column and colored region.
  2. Crowns can’t touch — not even diagonally.
  3. Beat your best time.
Play GridSpark
BorderRush

A geography pathfinding game.

  1. Name countries to build a land route from start to destination.
  2. Each correct guess lights up on the map.
  3. Fewer guesses, better score.
Play BorderRush
DropStack

A merge-drop arcade puzzle.

  1. Drop fruit into the basket; two of a kind merge into a bigger one.
  2. Don’t overflow the line at the top.
  3. Chase the melon — and the daily high score.
Play DropStack
Interrogate

An AI detective mystery.

  1. Read today’s case file, then question the suspects — they answer in character.
  2. Spot contradictions in their stories.
  3. Accuse the killer when you’re sure. One shot.
Play Interrogate
EpochLens

A when-and-where photo guessing game.

  1. Study each historical photo.
  2. Guess the year on the timeline and the place on the map.
  3. Closer guesses score more across 5 rounds.
Play EpochLens
